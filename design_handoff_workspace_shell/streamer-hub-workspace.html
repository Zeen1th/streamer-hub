Support Streamer Hub

If you enjoy Streamer Hub, please consider supporting its development. It stays local-first and free for everyone.

Why donate?

  • Keep the updates coming. Your support maintains continued development and ongoing improvements, ensuring it stays a powerful tool for streamers like you.
  • Early access. Gain early access to new features and updates before they are released to the public.
  • Supporter perks. Receive access to additional supporter benefits, including custom webhooks and cloud backups.
  • Show your support. Your support keeps Streamer Hub free, and shows your appreciation for the work that goes into it.
